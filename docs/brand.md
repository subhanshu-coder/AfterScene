# Afterscene brand

## Core idea

Afterscene is the conversation, memory, and community that lives on after the credits. It helps people discover films through their own taste and the people they trust.

## Voice

Warm, film-literate, curious, and personal. Talk about stories and people before algorithms. Keep recommendations spoiler-aware and explain why each one fits.

## Visual identity

- Wordmark: lowercase “afterscene.” in Manrope ExtraBold; use the original clapper mark alongside it.
- Display type: Manrope. Interface type: DM Sans.
- Base: near-black `#10110f`; surfaces: charcoal `#171815` and `#1d1e1a`.
- Accent: electric lime `#d4f36a`; reserve it for key actions and taste signals.
- Corners: 8–13 px for controls and cards; use clear borders instead of heavy glass blur.
- Iconography: Lucide line icons plus the custom clapperboard brand mark.

## Asset rules

The favicon and installable app icon are original vector assets in `frontend/public`. Movie imagery comes from TMDB and must retain its attribution. Do not use studio marks, film franchise logos, or fan art as Afterscene branding.
